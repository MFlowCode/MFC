Performance history

Grind time of each benchmark case on the CI machines, for every benchmarked commit on master. Phoenix is shown per GPU model, since its nodes differ several-fold. Grind time is nanoseconds per grid point, equation and Runge–Kutta stage; lower is faster. Nodes are shared, so single points are noisy: the line is a median of five runs, the dots are the runs themselves (a commit may have several). A dashed line marks a commit that changed the benchmark case itself, so the numbers on either side of it are not comparable.

Drag across a chart to zoom all of them; click a point to keep its commit and pull request.
